About the game

First off, let me introduce myself. I’m Ghhnu, and I started playing Rucoy about 9 years ago. Ever since I began creating content on YouTube, I always wanted to make a game similar to Rucoy, or be part of Rucoy itself. Unfortunately, the second one didn’t work out, but I made the first one a reality.

The gameplay is like Rucoy. There’s not much to explain, better that you discover it for yourselves.

Always offline

I want to make one thing clear: this game will never be online. If you want to play something online, go play Rucoy. This will always be offline.

Your progress

Your progress is saved on your device. When I announce an update, don’t uninstall the game. Just download the new APK and an “Update” button will appear. The moment you uninstall the game from your phone, your progress ceases to exist.
